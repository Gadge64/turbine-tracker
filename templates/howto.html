How to Use Turbine Tracker

{# ── Getting started ── #}

Getting started

Once you log in, you land on your personal Dashboard. If this is your first visit, it will be empty — that's normal. Follow the steps below to start populating your data.

The site works on any device — phone, tablet, or desktop — and you can log in from anywhere.

{# ── Step 1: Set up your profile ── #}

Step 1 — Set up your profile

Before adding readings, fill in your turbine details so the rest of the group can see what equipment you're running.

  1. Click Profile in the top menu
  2. Click Edit Profile
  3. Fill in your turbine model, capacity (kW), inverter model, and installation date
  4. Add your address and MPRN if you're happy for other members to see them
  5. Click Save

Your installation date helps the site calculate missing months and keep your records complete.

{# ── Step 2: Add your first monthly entry ── #}

Step 2 — Add your first monthly entry

Each month you record four meter readings. These are the numbers shown on the physical meters on your property.

Where to find the readings

  • Import meter — the ESB meter on your wall that counts electricity bought from the grid. Write down the reading at the start of the month, then again at the end.
  • Export meter — counts electricity sent back to the grid. Same process — start and end of month.
  • E-mon reading — the energy monitor attached to your turbine. This shows how many kWh your turbine generated this month (not a running total — just this month's figure).
  • Tariff rate — your current electricity price in €/kWh. Check your electricity bill. A typical rate is around €0.40/kWh.

How to add an entry

  1. Click Add Entry in the top menu
  2. Select the correct year and month
  3. Enter your import meter reading at the start of that month, then at the end
  4. Do the same for your export meter
  5. Enter your E-mon kWh figure for the month
  6. Enter your tariff rate (or leave it — it will default to the last rate you used)
  7. Click Save

The site automatically calculates your totals, self-sufficiency percentage, and euro value from these four figures. You don't need to do any maths yourself.

{# ── Step 3: Reading your dashboard ── #}

Step 3 — Reading your dashboard

Once you have a few months of data entered, your dashboard will show:

  • Generation chart — how many kWh your turbine produced each month
  • Self-sufficiency % — what percentage of your home's electricity came from your own turbine (higher is better)
  • Value @ tariff — estimated euro value of the electricity your turbine generated
  • CO₂ saved — estimated carbon offset based on your generation
  • Year tables — a full breakdown by month for each year
  • Missing months — if any months are gaps in your records, they'll be flagged so you can go back and fill them in
{# ── Step 4: Logging maintenance ── #}

Step 4 — Logging maintenance

Use the Maintenance page to keep a record of any servicing, inspections, or repairs done on your turbine.

  1. Click Maintenance in the top menu
  2. Click Add entry
  3. Enter the date, a description of the work done, and the cost (optional)
  4. If you know when the next service is due, enter that date too — you'll get a reminder on your dashboard
{# ── Comparing with others ── #}

Comparing with the group

The Compare page lets you plot your generation against any other member on the same chart — useful for spotting if your turbine is underperforming.

The Community page shows combined group totals and a breakdown by member for the selected year.

The Leaderboard ranks members by generation, self-sufficiency, and logging consistency.

{# ── Tips ── #}

Tips for keeping good records

  • Take your meter readings on the same day each month — the 1st is a good habit
  • If you miss a month, add it anyway — you can enter any past month at any time
  • Use the Notes field to flag anything unusual that month (e.g. turbine was offline for 2 weeks)
  • You can download all your data as a CSV file any time from your Dashboard → Export

⚙️ Admin guide — visible to admins only

Creating a new member account

  1. Go to Admin → Users
  2. Click Add new user
  3. Enter a username (first name in lowercase works well), a password, and their full name
  4. Click Save — the account is created immediately
  5. Their password will appear on the Credentials sheet so you can print it and hand it to them

Creating multiple accounts at once

  1. Go to Admin → Batch add users
  2. Paste a list in the format: Full Name, Turbine Model, Capacity kW — one person per line
  3. All accounts are created in one go with auto-generated usernames and passwords

Printing the credentials sheet

  1. Go to Admin → Users
  2. Click Credentials sheet
  3. Click Print / Save as PDF — the page formats itself cleanly for printing
  4. Note: if a member has changed their own password, their row will show "changed by user" — use Edit Profile → Set new password to refresh it

Adding entries on behalf of a member

  1. Go to Admin → Users and click Edit Profile for that member
  2. Or go to Members, click the member's name, then click Add entry for this user

Excluding an account from charts and comparisons

  1. Go to Admin → Users → Edit Profile for that account
  2. Tick Exclude from all charts & comparisons
  3. Click Save — that account will no longer appear in community totals, the compare page, or the leaderboard

Managing backups

  1. The database is backed up automatically every day — no action needed
  2. To download a backup manually, click Backup in the top menu
  3. To browse all backups, go to Admin → Backups
  4. If you ever need to restore from a backup, contact your site administrator
